# Iowa 2026 Poll: Sand Has an Edge in Governor's Race; Hinson Holds Narrow Senate Advantage

August 6th, 2026

A new Emerson College Polling/Nexstar Media survey of Iowa voters in the race for Governor finds 48% of voters support Democrat Rob Sand, while 43% support Republican Zach Lahn. Seven percent are undecided. In the race for U.S. Senate, 48% support Republican Ashley Hinson, while 45% support Democrat Josh Turek. Six percent are undecided.

**Methodology**

The Emerson College Polling Iowa survey was conducted August 2-4, 2026. The sample of likely voters, n=712 has a credibility interval, similar to a poll's margin of error (MOE), of +/- 3.7 percent. The data sets were weighted by gender, education, race, age, party registration, and region based on U.S. Census parameters and voter file data.
